About us

Applied robotics, built in Italy.

Abra Robotics was founded to bring the most advanced robotics where it truly matters: Italian companies looking to automate, and research laboratories working on the systems of the future. We select the best hardware in the world, configure it for your use case, and support you from assessment to go-live.

Book a call Discover our values

Our mission

No top-down projects. No hard sells.

We work with companies and research institutes that want to integrate robotics into their processes. We always start from the real context: what you need to do, with what constraints, over what time horizon. Only then do we propose a solution, and only if it makes sense.

Our values

What makes us different.

Three principles that guide every project.

Process-first approach.

We start from your real process, simulate in 3D, then propose. No robots sold without an assessment.

Best-in-class hardware.

We distribute only brands we consider among the best in the world for industry and research: Unitree, Universal Robots, MiR, OnRobot, Robotiq, Keyence.

Support after go-live.

The robot arrives at your facility? Good, the work starts now. Configuration, training, maintenance, and continuous optimization.
