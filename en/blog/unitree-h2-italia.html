Unitree H2 in the UK: Air, EDU, H2-D and H2 Plus compared

29 July 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: the H2 is Unitree's full-size humanoid: 182 cm, about 70 kg, 31 degrees of freedom and about 3 hours of runtime. The price list has the H2 Air (€43,874), H2 EDU (€68,085), H2-D (from €79,900) and H2 Plus (from €149,900), excluding VAT. If you need to write code, you need the H2 EDU. The H2 Plus is the reference version for NVIDIA Isaac GR00T and is quoted on request.

The Unitree H2 is not a taller G1. At adult height its relationship with the environment changes: doors, benches, workstations and tools designed for people can be used without adaptation, and torque of up to 360 N·m at the leg joints allows loads that a 132 cm robot cannot handle. Weight, floor space and the risks of a fall change too. All versions, with product pages and prices, are on the H2 range page.

Which H2 to choose

  • H2 Air: walks, interacts and is controlled with Unitree software. It is meant for presence, events and human-scale demonstrations. No secondary development: you cannot install your own code.
  • H2 EDU: the version for universities, research centres and companies that develop their own software. It adds the Intel Core i7 development PC, SDK access and the option to fit hands and computing modules. 12-month warranty instead of 8.
  • H2-D: does not walk. It is the H2 torso with two arms on a fixed or mobile base, designed for manipulation in a cell and for collecting teleoperation data. It removes the risk of a fall and is close to a manipulation cell. A more compact alternative is the G1-D.
  • H2 Plus: H2 body, NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor T5000 computing and two five-finger Sharpa Wave tactile hands. It is the platform designed for NVIDIA Isaac GR00T foundation models.

The G1 remains the platform with the most published work and the most available code: for a lab that is just starting, it is almost always the place to begin. The H2 makes sense when the task really needs human scale. The comparison with the R1 and G1-D is in the 2026 humanoid comparison.

H2 Plus and Isaac GR00T: what to expect

According to the NVIDIA announcement of 31 May 2026, the H2 Plus is the reference humanoid for the Isaac GR00T platform, sold through Unitree from late 2026. With us it is on request: availability and lead times are confirmed in the offer.

Two things to know before committing to this version. First: a foundation model does not work straight out of the box. The work by Park, Kee and Oh (arXiv:2512.01358) shows that on the G1 a grasping task goes from 48% to 94% success only after fine-tuning on the robot's own data. Second: the GR00T results published so far concern the G1 and other robots, not the H2. That is why our quotes always separate three items: the hardware platform, data collection and training, and safety and the cell.

What the G1 research teaches anyone buying an H2

Preprints on Unitree humanoids almost always deal with the G1. Three lessons carry over to the H2 as they are:

  • Runtime depends on the task. Deniz et al. (arXiv:2606.15915) model the power draw of the G1 arm: with continuous manipulation, the H2's declared 3 hours must be measured again on your own cycle.
  • Test teleoperation in simulation first. Durrani and Khan (arXiv:2605.12347) drive a G1 with a sensor suit, moving from MuJoCo to the robot: motion retargeting, latency and the safety harness are the three points of every demo.
  • Many failures are not hardware. Amadio and Mingo Hoffman (arXiv:2607.19903) show that in human motion tracking the way commands are represented matters: it is worth settling those choices before blaming the robot.

Other papers, with what they mean for buyers, are in our selection of arXiv papers on Unitree.

Safety and regulations

With about 70 kg and 360 N·m at the legs, an H2 fall is a serious risk for people and for the robot. Before use you need a written risk assessment, a frame or harness for the first tests and a dedicated area with controlled access. In the EU, from 20 January 2027 the reference for machinery is Regulation (EU) 2023/1230: what changes is in the guide to Unitree robots and the Machinery Regulation. The method for setting up a trial in a company is in the guide to a PoC with a humanoid.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree H2 cost in the UK?

At list price the H2 Air is €43,874 and the H2 EDU €68,085, excluding VAT. The H2-D starts from €79,900 and the H2 Plus from €149,900, with the final price depending on the configuration.

How tall is the H2 and how much does it weigh?

According to Unitree, 1820 mm standing and about 70 kg with battery, with 31 degrees of freedom.

How long does the H2 battery last?

About 3 hours according to Unitree, with a 15 Ah (0.972 kWh) battery.

What is the difference between the H2 and the H2 EDU?

Secondary development is available only on the H2 EDU, which adds an Intel Core i7 development PC. The Unitree warranty is 8 months for the H2 and 12 months for the H2 EDU.

What is the H2 Plus?

It is the reference version for NVIDIA Isaac GR00T, with Jetson AGX Thor T5000 and Sharpa Wave tactile hands. NVIDIA indicates sales through Unitree from late 2026.
