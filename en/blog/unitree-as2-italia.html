Unitree AS2 in the UK: versions, prices and how to choose

29 July 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: the AS2 is the Unitree quadruped that sits between the Go2 and the A2: about 20 kg, up to 15 kg of payload in motion and IP54 protection on the Pro, X and EDU versions. List prices run from €5,400 (AS2 Air) to €21,277 (AS2 EDU Flagship), excluding VAT; the AS2 Pro and AS2-W are quoted on request. If you need to write code, choose the X or EDU; for surveillance with the app the Pro is enough; the Air is for demos and light indoor inspection.

Anyone weighing up a Unitree quadruped today is no longer choosing only between the Go2 and the A2. The AS2 series sits in between: the same compact format as the Go2, but with more payload, protection against dust and splashes and a higher grade of sensors. It is the right size for professional demos, for labs that want to get out of the test room, and for inspection or patrol work where the Go2 is not enough and the A2 or B2 would be too much.

The Air has the standard 8000 mAh battery (over 1.5 hours with a 10 kg payload) and the Unitree L2 LiDAR; the Pro, X and EDU have the 15000 mAh battery (over 2.5 hours with 13-15 kg) and an industrial LiDAR with 64-128 lines. Unitree notes that the values are measured in the laboratory and can vary with payload and terrain.

Which AS2 to choose

  • AS2 Air, €5,400. Driven with the app and the remote control, carries about 10 kg and has no declared IP rating. Good for trade shows, classrooms and indoor site visits. It cannot be programmed.
  • AS2 X, €10,271. It has the numbers of the top versions (15 kg, IP54, secondary development) with a more basic kit than the EDU. It is the cheapest way to write code on an AS2.
  • AS2 EDU, from €12,473. The version for labs and integrators: SDK, ROS 2, charging dock and, as you move up the tiers, extra computing and LiDAR. The exact contents of U1-U4 are on the product pages.
  • AS2 Pro, on request. Designed for surveillance and inspection with the Unitree app: wide-angle LiDAR and front and rear cameras already fitted. No secondary development: if the data has to feed your own software, you need the X or the EDU.

AS2, Go2 or A2?

  • Go2: costs less (from €2,576), has the largest research community and the most open-source papers and policies to reproduce. No declared IP rating and 1-2 hours of runtime on the Air and Pro. Details in Go2 vs Go2 Pro.
  • AS2: the same format, but 13-15 kg of payload, IP54 and better sensors. It is the choice for customer demos, indoor patrols and inspection PoCs that do not need an industrial-size robot.
  • A2 and B2: plant-grade payloads and protection. The A2 is rated IP56 (A2 Pro up to IP67 on the main components) with about 25 kg of payload in motion; the B2 is IP67 with over 40 kg (A2 page, B2 page). The comparison for inspection work is in the guide to quadrupeds for industrial inspection.

What the research says (and why it talks about the Go2)

Papers published on arXiv almost always use the Go2, because it is the most common quadruped in labs. The methods also apply to a PoC with an AS2; the numbers do not, and must be measured again. Three useful papers:

  • AcL (arXiv:2503.21401) trains gaits that cope with up to four failed joints and tests them on a real Go2. It suggests an acceptance test: what the robot does if a motor locks up mid-route.
  • MoE + RoboGauge (arXiv:2602.00678) proposes metrics to predict how well a policy trained in simulation will hold up on the robot, with tests on snow, sand and stairs. It helps you discard weak policies before physical trials.
  • Li et al. (arXiv:2606.03340) run a Go2 EDU through a library with ROS 2, RTAB-Map and Nav2: 100% of routes completed with a static scene, 88% with many people around. It is the closest reference to an indoor patrol.

Other papers, with what they mean for buyers, are in our selection of arXiv papers on Unitree.

Software: what to expect

On the X and EDU versions you work with unitree_sdk2 and, for ROS 2, with unitree_ros2 on Ubuntu 22.04 and ROS 2 Humble. As of October 2026 the unitree_ros2 repository does not yet list the AS2 among the robots with dedicated examples: before a ROS 2-only project on the AS2, check with us the state of support for the firmware version being delivered. Installation and the first topic are in the ROS 2 quick-start guide.

Next steps

  1. Choose the version on the basis of three questions: do you need to write code? Will the robot work around dust or splashes? How much do the sensors you want to fit weigh?
  2. If the use case is not clear, the "Find the right robot" questionnaire is there for that.
  3. For a PoC, set the metrics first: routes completed, operator interventions, hours of runtime with the real payload.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree AS2 cost in the UK?

List prices start at €5,400 for the AS2 Air; AS2-X €10,271, AS2 EDU from €12,473 to €21,277, AS2 Pro on request. Excluding VAT.

How much payload can the AS2 carry?

According to Unitree, about 10 kg in continuous motion for the Air, 13 kg for the Pro and 15 kg for the X and EDU. Standing still, up to about 65 kg on the X and EDU versions.

Is the AS2 waterproof?

The Pro, X and EDU are rated IP54 (dust and splashes). For the AS2 Air, Unitree does not declare an IP rating.

AS2 or Go2 for a lab?

The Go2 EDU has more open-source code and papers to reproduce. The AS2 EDU is the better buy if you need more payload, IP54 and a sturdier platform for tests outside the lab.
