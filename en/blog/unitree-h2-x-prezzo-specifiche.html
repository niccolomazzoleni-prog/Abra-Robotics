Unitree H2-X: price, specs and differences from the H2

26 November 2026Abra Robotics
In short: the Unitree H2-X (also written H2X) is Unitree's new full-size humanoid for security and rescue, and it is a different robot from the H2: about 170 cm, 65+ kg, 31 degrees of freedom, two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries and about 8 hours of runtime even while walking. It starts at €36,713Unitree H2-X€36,713 excl. VATView product → excl. VAT for the H2-X Air; the H2-X EDU with the SDK costs €54,057. It is on pre-order.

What the Unitree H2-X is

Unitree unveiled the H2-X in October 2026 with the video "Kung Fu Legend: Guardian", about security and rescue. On its official page Unitree describes it as a next-generation full-size humanoid for security, manufacturing, commercial services and home services.

It is not a version of the H2: it is a different robot, more compact, with almost three times the runtime and two batteries you can swap without switching it off. In the video the robot stops an attacker, breaks a car door and lifts a vehicle to free a person. Unitree has not said which of these actions are autonomous.

Who the H2-X is for

For anyone who needs a humanoid on duty for many hours: security patrols, reception, monitoring spaces, emergency-scenario trials. The Air runs with the remote and app; to develop your own software you need the EDU with the SDK.

Unitree recommends keeping at least 2–3 metres of safety distance from the robot. Security or rescue use needs a site risk assessment before installation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree H2-X cost?

From €36,713 excl. VAT for the H2-X Air. The H2-X EDU with the SDK costs €54,057; with hands it ranges from €55,378 to €156,772. The final price is confirmed in the written quote.

Are the H2-X and the H2 the same robot?

No. The H2-X is a different robot: about 170 cm and 65+ kg versus 182 cm and about 70 kg for the H2, two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries and about 8 hours of runtime versus about 3 hours, more arm torque and an LED display in the head.

How long does the H2-X battery last?

Unitree states about 8 hours both standing and walking, with two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries and magnetic charging.

Which H2-X configuration should I choose?

Air for demos and remote-controlled use; EDU to develop software with the SDK. Choose the hands by task, from Dex1-1 grippers to visuo-tactile BrainCo Revo 3 U21V hands.

When will the H2-X ship?

It is on pre-order. The delivery date is confirmed in the quote after checking with Unitree.
