Unitree Robotics: who they are, what they make and how to buy

Published 13 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: Unitree Robotics is the Chinese company Hangzhou Yushu Technology Co., Ltd., founded in August 2016 in Hangzhou by Wang Xingxing, which makes quadruped and humanoid robots. Since 19 August 2026 it has been listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (STAR Market, ticker 688836). Abra Robotics supplies the Unitree range in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English.

This page collects verified facts about Unitree Robotics: who they are, where they come from, what they make, the state of the stock market listing and how to buy the robots.

This page reports facts verified against official sources and reputable press. The section on the listing is for information only and is not investment advice.

Who Unitree Robotics are

Unitree Robotics is the trading name of Hangzhou Yushu Technology Co., Ltd. (杭州宇树科技有限公司), a robotics company based in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China. The operating address given by the manufacturer is Fengda Creative Park, Binjiang District, Hangzhou.

The company was founded on 26 August 2016 by Wang Xingxing. The story starts with the XDog quadruped robot, which Wang developed during his master's studies at Shanghai University. The XDog videos went viral, attracting buyers and investors while Wang had only recently joined DJI. He left DJI and, with angel investment, founded Unitree in a 50 square metre office in Binjiang, Hangzhou.

The first product was Laikago (2017), a quadruped. According to Wikipedia, in 2025 the company had 500 employees and revenue of 1.70 billion yuan (250 million US dollars).

What Unitree Robotics make

The current range is split into three main families, all listed on the official website:

  • Quadrupeds: Go1 and Go2 for consumers; As2, A2, B2 (including B2-W), B1 and Aliengo for education and industry. You will find them on our quadrupeds page, with product pages and prices.
  • Humanoids: G1, H1, H2 (including H2 Plus), R1 and R1-D. See the humanoids page.
  • Arms and sensors: Z1 arm, 7-axis bionic arm, 4D LiDAR L1 and L2.

The development timeline is documented on the official website: Laikago (2017), Aliengo (2019), A1 (2020, presented at CES), Go1 (2021), Go2 (2023). The humanoids came later: the H1 was the first, then the G1 (2024) and, in 2025, the H2 and the R1.

Is Unitree Robotics listed on the stock market?

Yes, since 19 August 2026. The shares are listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, on the STAR Market (Science and Technology Innovation Board), under ticker 688836. Here are the facts and dates, as reported by the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the BBC:

  • 20 March 2026: the Shanghai Stock Exchange accepts the IPO application.
  • 1 June 2026: the exchange's listing committee reviews the application.
  • Offer price: 150.8 yuan per share, with about 40.4 million new shares.
  • 19 August 2026: first day of trading. The stock closes at 845 yuan, up 460% on the offer price.

How do you buy Unitree Robotics shares? They are A-shares listed in Shanghai: for a UK or international investor, access goes through a broker authorised for the Chinese market, with the restrictions and risks that entails. We do not give investment advice and we do not sell financial products.

How much does the Unitree G1 cost

The G1 is Unitree's compact humanoid. The family runs from €19,809 for the G1 Basic (G1 Air) to €90,829 for the G1 Edu Ultimate I, excluding VAT. The price depends on the version: the G1 Air is the entry level, while the Edu versions add degrees of freedom and computing power. The full product pages are in the Unitree catalogue.
