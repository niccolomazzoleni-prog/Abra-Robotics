Unitree Robotics: who they are, what they make and how to buy

13 October 2026Updated 19 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: Unitree Robotics is the Chinese company Hangzhou Yushu Technology Co., Ltd., founded in 2016 in Hangzhou by Wang Xingxing. It makes quadruped and humanoid robots and has been listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange since August 2026. Abra Robotics sells its robots with a public price list in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English.

The section on the stock market listing is for information only and is not investment advice.

Who Unitree Robotics are

Unitree Robotics is the trading name of Hangzhou Yushu Technology Co., Ltd. (杭州宇树科技有限公司), a robotics company based in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China. The operating address given by the manufacturer is Fengda Creative Park, Binjiang District, Hangzhou.

The company was founded in 2016 by Wang Xingxing. The story starts with XDog, a quadruped Wang built during his postgraduate studies between 2013 and 2016. After leaving DJI, Wang founded Unitree with money from a business angel.

The first commercial product was the Laikago quadruped, in September 2017. According to the listing prospectus, Unitree had revenue of 1.70 billion yuan in 2025, with a net profit of 278 million yuan.

What Unitree Robotics make

The official store splits the range into three families:

  • Quadrupeds: Go2 for personal use and education; AS2, A2 and B2 (including B2-W) for research and industry. The official store also still lists the older Go1, B1 and Aliengo, which are not in our price list. Product pages and prices are on the quadrupeds page.
  • Humanoids: G1, H1, H2 (including H2 Plus), R1 and R1-D. Product pages and prices are on the humanoids page.
  • Arms and sensors: Z1 arm, 7-axis bionic arm, 4D LiDAR L1 and L2.

The quadruped timeline on the official page: Laikago (2017), Aliengo (2019), A1 (presented at CES in January 2020), Go1 (2021). 2023 brought the Go2 and the first humanoid, the H1. The G1 followed (2024) and, in 2025, the H2 and the R1.

Is Unitree Robotics listed on the stock market?

Yes, since 19 August 2026. The shares are listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, on the STAR Market (Science and Technology Innovation Board), under ticker 688836. The milestones, according to the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the international press:

  • 20 March 2026: the Shanghai Stock Exchange accepts the IPO application.
  • 1 June 2026: the exchange's listing committee approves the application, 73 days after it was filed.
  • Offer price: 150.8 yuan per share, for at least 40.4 million new shares.
  • 19 August 2026: first day of trading. The stock closes at 845 yuan, up 460% on the offer price.

How much does the Unitree G1 cost

The G1 is Unitree's compact humanoid. The family runs from €19,809 for the G1 Basic (G1 Air) to €90,829 for the G1 Edu Ultimate I, excluding VAT. The G1 Air is the base version; the Edu versions add the SDK, more degrees of freedom, more computing power and, in the higher configurations, robotic hands. The full product pages are in the Unitree catalogue.

Frequently asked questions

Who are Unitree Robotics?

A Chinese robotics company based in Hangzhou, founded in 2016 by Wang Xingxing. It makes quadrupeds and, since 2023, humanoids, starting with the H1.

Is Unitree Robotics listed on the stock market?

Yes, since 19 August 2026, on the STAR Market of the Shanghai Stock Exchange under ticker 688836. The offer price was 150.8 yuan per share.

How do you buy Unitree Robotics shares?

They are A-shares on the Shanghai Stock Exchange: a UK or international investor has to go through a broker authorised for the Chinese market, with the restrictions and risks that entails. Abra Robotics does not give investment advice and does not sell financial products.

What do Unitree Robotics make?

Three families: quadrupeds (the Go2 for personal use; the AS2, A2 and B2 for education and industry), humanoids (G1, H1, H2, R1) and components such as the Z1 arm and the L1 and L2 4D LiDARs.

How much does the Unitree G1 cost?

From €19,809 to €90,829, excluding VAT, according to the public Abra Robotics price list. The price depends on the version and the accessories: the base is the G1 Basic (G1 Air), and the Edu versions add the SDK and computing power.
