Humanoid robot price: what they really cost in 2026

9 October 2026Updated 19 October 2026Abra Robotics

In short. A complete Unitree humanoid that walks on its own legs costs from €7,190 (R1 Air) to €261,776 (H2 Plus Ultimate N), excluding VAT. Torso versions without legs start at €6,295. The price is driven by size, degrees of freedom, hands and whether it can be programmed with the SDK. No humanoid on the price list is a domestic robot.

The price of a humanoid robot depends on what you mean by "humanoid". The cheapest item on the public price list for the Unitree range is the R1-A5 Standard: a torso with arms and head, about 11 kg, powered externally, that does not walk. The most expensive is the H2 Plus Ultimate N, a configuration of the full-size H2. All prices are excluding VAT. Shipping and insurance are quoted in the offer; import VAT and duty are described on our UK page.

What changes from one band to the next

The R1 is the entry point of the range. According to the official Unitree spec sheet it is 123 cm tall and weighs about 29 kg (27 kg for the Air), with 20 degrees of freedom in the Air, 26 in the base version and 26 to 40 in the EDU. SDK programming and the optional compute module (Orin, 40 to 100 TOPS) are available only on the R1 EDU. Rated battery life is about 1 hour. It suits a limited budget and a start with courses and first trials.

The G1 is 132 cm tall, weighs about 35 kg and has 23 to 43 degrees of freedom according to the Unitree G1 spec sheet. The real difference between the base G1 and the G1 EDU is SDK programming, available only on the EDU. Knee torque (90 vs 120 N·m), arm payload (about 2 vs about 3 kg) and warranty (8 vs 18 months) also change. Air, EDU and G1-D are compared in which G1 to choose.

The G1-D has no legs: it is a torso with two 7-axis arms on a fixed stand (Standard) or on a wheeled mobile base (Flagship). It is for tasks that involve handling objects on a bench, not walking.

The H2 is the full-size humanoid: about 182 cm and 70 kg, 31 degrees of freedom and up to 360 N·m of torque at the leg joints. Check the equipment carefully. According to the Unitree H2 page, the standard version comes only with the PC1 Intel Core i5 (platform functions). The Intel Core i7 (PC2, custom development), the dexterous hands and SDK programming belong to the H2 EDU, which also has a 12-month warranty instead of 8. The price list also includes an optional computing board, the H2 Jetson AGX Thor Computing Board at €17,608.

Is there a humanoid robot for the home?

The humanoids on our price list are not domestic robots: the R1, G1, G1-D and H2 are built for companies, universities and laboratories, and need someone to set them up and supervise them. The models presented "for the home" today are pre-orders not available in the UK, or promises.

1X NEO. The NEO can be pre-ordered on the 1X order page with a refundable deposit of $200. The price is $20,000 to buy in Early Access, or $499 a month on subscription. As of October 2026 the page still lists US deliveries "starting 2026", with no month given, and no other country. There is no offer for the UK.

Tesla Optimus. No published price, no active sales. At the World Economic Forum in Davos, in January 2026, Elon Musk said he expects Tesla to sell humanoid robots to the public by the end of 2027, as reported by Fox Business. It is a forecast, not a product you can order.

What is the most advanced humanoid robot?

Among those you can buy from the price list, the largest and most powerful is the H2. But "most advanced" does not mean "right for the project": a lab studying locomotion often does better starting with a G1 EDU, and anyone who needs to handle objects on a bench will find a G1-D more useful. The point-by-point comparison is in the 2026 humanoid comparison; to work out whether a humanoid makes sense in your company before buying one, there is the guide to a PoC with a humanoid robot.

What to consider beyond the price

  • Custom software. On the R1, G1 and H2 you need an EDU version to run your own code with the SDK. The base versions run only Unitree's software.
  • Hands. On the price list a single BrainCo Revo 2 Basic hand for the R1 costs €5,356, and a robot normally has two: the pair costs more than an R1 Air. If the project is not about grasping, start without them.
  • Accessories. Spare batteries, protective frames and computing modules have their own prices on the list.
  • Financing. Before the quote we can prepare the technical documentation for your claim under UK schemes such as capital allowances and R&D tax relief; we do not give tax advice. Details are on the Financing page.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a humanoid robot cost in 2026?

A Unitree humanoid that walks costs from €7,190 (R1 Air) to €261,776 (H2 Plus Ultimate N), excluding VAT. The G1 starts at €19,809, the H2 at €43,874. Torsos without legs start at €6,295.

Is there a humanoid robot for the home?

Not among those on our price list: the R1, G1, G1-D and H2 are built for companies, universities and laboratories. The domestic models announced so far, such as the 1X NEO and Tesla Optimus, cannot be ordered in the UK as of October 2026.

What is the most advanced humanoid robot?

Among those on the price list, the largest and most powerful is the H2: full-size, with up to 360 N·m of torque in the legs. Dexterous hands and your own code require the H2 EDU. For locomotion or bench manipulation a G1 EDU or a G1-D is often enough.

Where can I buy a humanoid robot in the UK?

Abra Robotics supplies the Unitree range in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English, and publishes the prices of the R1, G1, G1-D and H2 online. For a quote, start from the Find the right robot questionnaire.

How much does the Tesla Optimus humanoid robot cost?

As of October 2026 Tesla has not published a price or opened sales. In Davos, in January 2026, Elon Musk said he expects public sales by the end of 2027: it is a forecast.

How much does the 1X NEO humanoid robot cost?

According to the 1X order page, $20,000 in Early Access or $499 a month on subscription. As of October 2026 the deliveries listed are in the United States only; it is not on sale in the UK.
