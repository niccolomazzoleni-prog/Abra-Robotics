Unitree AS2 vs Go2: differences, prices and which to choose

25 November 2026Abra Robotics
In short: the AS2 is Unitree's newer quadruped in the Go2 class and beats it on almost everything: up to 15 kg of walking payload against about 8, IP54 on the Pro, X and EDU (the Go2 has no declared IP rating), joint torque up to 95 N·m against 45, an industrial 64-128-line LiDAR, about 4 hours of runtime and operation from -20 °C. It costs more: the AS2 X is €10,271 against €6,398 for the Go2 X, excl. VAT. The Go2 remains the right choice for demos, teaching and clean indoor spaces.

If you are looking for a Unitree robot dog for work, you no longer have to start from the Go2. The Unitree AS2 came later, with a body of about 20 kg designed to carry real sensors, go outdoors and last an inspection shift. The Go2, at about 15 kg, remains the most widespread quadruped for classrooms, labs and demos. Here we compare them version by version, with data from Unitree's official spec sheets and prices from the Abra price list.

Where the AS2 beats the Go2

  • Payload. With 15 kg while walking (X and EDU) the AS2 carries a 3D LiDAR, a thermal camera or a light arm with its brackets. On the Go2, with about 8 kg, the sensor and its mount use up almost all the margin.
  • Environment. IP54 means protection against dust and splashes: dusty warehouses, yards, light rain. The Go2 declares neither an IP rating nor an operating temperature; the AS2 works from -20 °C.
  • Runtime under load. The AS2 Pro, X and EDU declare over 2.5 hours with 13-15 kg on board. The Go2 declares no runtime under load.
  • Perception. The industrial 64-128-line LiDAR on the Pro, X and EDU gives denser maps than the L2, useful for SLAM and navigation in large spaces.
  • Steps and joints. 25 cm stairs against about 16, and almost twice the joint torque: more margin on gratings, ramps and obstacles.
  • Development. The AS2 X supports secondary development; the Go2 X only partly. To write code on a robust quadruped, the AS2 X is the entry point.

When the Go2 is still enough

The Go2 is not a model to dismiss. It is the right choice for trade fairs, showrooms and teaching, where the entry price matters; for research labs working on locomotion and reinforcement learning, where it is light and simulated in Unitree's official projects; and for indoor surveys with sensors under 8 kg. The differences between Go2 versions are in the guide Go2 vs Go2 Pro.

The rule of thumb: if the specification asks for an IP rating, a minimum temperature or more than 8 kg of sensors, rule out the Go2 and start from the AS2. If the route is outdoors with mud or water, or the sensors exceed 15 kg, move up to the A2 or the B2: the inspection comparison is in AS2, Go2 or B2.

The real cost: charging and development

For autonomous use, with the robot going back to charge on its own, you need the charging dock: it is included with the AS2 EDU, not with the AS2 X; the Go2 X and EDU are compatible with Unitree's dock, bought separately. Quadrupeds ship without a ready-made application: map, mission, sensor reading and integration with company systems are development work. That is where most of the budget goes, and where we work, starting from a digital twin of the route before the purchase.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the Unitree AS2 and Go2?

The AS2 is the newer, tougher quadruped: about 20 kg against 15, up to 15 kg of walking payload against about 8, IP54 on the Pro, X and EDU versions (the Go2 has no declared IP rating), joint torque up to 95 N·m against 45 and an industrial 64-128-line LiDAR. The Go2 remains lighter and cheaper.

Does the AS2 replace the Go2?

For professional work, yes: outdoors, dust, light rain, heavy sensors and long rounds. The Go2 remains the choice for demos, teaching and clean indoor spaces when budget matters more than performance.

How much does the AS2 cost compared with the Go2?

In the Abra price list, excl. VAT: AS2 Air €5,400 against Go2 Air €2,576, AS2 X €10,271.49 against Go2 X €6,398.39, AS2 EDU from €12,472.53 against Go2 EDU from €8,804.14.

Which AS2 can be programmed?

The X and EDU versions: Unitree lists secondary development as supported on both and not supported on the Air and Pro. The EDU adds the charging dock and the expansion dock for a compute module such as an Orin NX.

Can the AS2 be used in the rain?

The Pro, X and EDU versions are rated IP54: protected against dust and water splashes, so light rain yes, immersion and powerful jets no. The AS2 Air and the whole Go2 range have no declared IP rating.
