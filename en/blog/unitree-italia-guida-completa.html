Where can you buy Unitree?

Abra Robotics (Niccolò Mazzoleni, VAT no. IT04800170278) supplies the Unitree range in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English, and works as an integrator for Italy and selected European markets.

  • Registered office: Viale Trieste 105, 30026 Portogruaro (VE), Italy

Why a supplier with technical support matters: up-to-date firmware, warranty, original spare parts, correct configuration at first start-up and a technical contact who speaks English when the robot is in production or in the lab.

How to buy a Unitree robot from Abra

  1. Assessment: online questionnaire or an introductory call to understand the use case, budget and constraints (ROS 2, IP rating, payload).
  2. Quote: SKU configuration, accessories, any custom software and lead time.
  3. Financing: a free technical check for UK schemes such as capital allowances and R&D tax relief, or for leasing; we provide the quote and technical documentation, not tax advice (our financing page covers the Italian schemes, for comparison).
  4. Delivery and support: configured robot, start-up session, documentation in English where available.

Indicative lead times from signing the order: quadrupeds 4-6 weeks, humanoids 4-6 weeks (they vary by SKU and accessories and are confirmed in the written quote).

Which Unitree should you choose? Decision tree

  1. Inspection or surveillance only, with no development? Go2 Pro or AS2 Pro
  2. University lab, ROS 2, thesis work? Go2 EDU, G1-U1/U2, R1 EDU or H2 EDU
  3. Bimanual manipulation on the factory floor (pilot)? G1 EDU with hands (U3 and up) or G1-D (from €38,005)
  4. Heavy industrial work outdoors? B2 or A2 Pro
  5. Limited budget but an open SDK? R1 EDU or Go2 EDU

Not sure? Try the Abra assessment or book a call with the technical team.

Frequently asked questions

Does Abra Robotics supply Unitree robots in the UK?

Yes. Abra Robotics supplies the Unitree range in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English: sales, configuration, training and technical support.

Can I buy just the robot, without services?

Yes, for teams that already have in-house expertise. We deliver the robot configured; support is optional.

Where can I find Unitree prices in euros?

In the Abra price list and on the product pages in the catalogue. Prices are indicative end-user prices, excluding VAT; shipping and insurance to the UK are quoted separately, and a GBP equivalent is available on request.

Can the Go2 X, G1-D and H2-D be ordered already?

Yes: they are in the price list with a product page and price (Go2-X €6,398, G1-D from €38,005, H2-D from €79,900, excluding VAT). Availability and lead times are confirmed in the quote.

What warranty do Unitree robots have?

According to Unitree's official product pages: G1 8 months and G1 EDU 18 months; H2 8 months and H2 EDU 12 months. For the other models, the warranty is stated in the offer.

Do you deliver to the UK?

Yes. We ship from Italy as an export, so no Italian VAT is charged. Goods are delivered DAP: the UK importer pays import VAT (20%) and any customs duty, and needs a GB EORI number. VAT-registered businesses can use postponed VAT accounting. Shipping and insurance are quoted in the offer.

Where can I buy Unitree in the UK and Europe?

From Abra Robotics: info@abrarobotics.com · English homepage.
