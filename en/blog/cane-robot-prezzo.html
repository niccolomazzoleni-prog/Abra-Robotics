Robot dog: prices and models you can buy in the UK

9 October 2026Updated 19 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: a Unitree robot dog starts at €2,576 excluding VAT (Go2 Air) and goes up to €104,182 (B2-W with LiDAR). The Go2 and AS2 cover education, research and professional use; the A2 and B2 are industrial quadrupeds: the A2 with about 25 kg of payload and IP56/IP67, the B2 with over 40 kg and IP67.

In this article a robot dog means a quadruped with LiDAR and a measurable payload, not a remote-controlled toy. Prices are taken from the Abra Robotics public price list, family by family.

Go2: the entry point for education and development

The Go2 weighs about 15 kg and carries an L2 4D LiDAR. Unitree does not declare an IP rating: it is fine indoors, in the lab and for demos, not in rain or dust.

VersionPrice (excl. VAT)
Go2 Air€2,576
Go2 Pro€3,981
Go2 EDU€8,804
Go2 EDU with Livox Mid-360 LiDAR€16,875

The Air is operated with the app and remote control and has a 6-month warranty. The Pro adds 4G with GPS, reaches 3.5 m/s instead of 2.5 and has a 12-month warranty. Only the EDU can be programmed with the SDK and ROS 2, and it has the 15000 mAh battery. The differences point by point are in Go2 vs Go2 Pro. If you need protection from dust and water or more payload, move up to the AS2.

Realistic robot dog or toy? How to tell them apart

A toy is a remote-controlled plastic device with no SDK, no LiDAR and no protection, costing a few tens of euros. A real quadruped has a measurable payload, a LiDAR for navigation, an SDK and ROS 2 on the development versions, and an IP rating on the A2, B2 and almost every AS2. If the price is under €1,000, it is almost always a toy.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a robot dog cost?

From €2,576 excluding VAT for the Go2 Air to €104,182 for the B2-W with LiDAR. The AS2 Pro is priced on request.

What is the difference between a toy robot dog and a professional one?

A toy is driven with a remote control and has no LiDAR, no SDK and no protection from dust and water. A Unitree quadruped has LiDAR, an SDK and ROS 2 on the development versions, and an IP rating on the A2, B2 and the AS2 Pro, X and EDU.

Which robot dog should you choose for a university or lab?

The Go2 EDU (from €8,804) or AS2 EDU (from €12,473): both are programmed with the SDK and ROS 2. The higher configurations add computing power and 3D LiDAR.

Which robot dog for industrial inspection?

The A2 (IP56, about 25 kg of payload) or B2 (IP67, over 40 kg) for plants with dust and water. In more sheltered environments the AS2 Pro, X and EDU (IP54) are enough.
