Robot dog: prices and models you can buy in the UK

9 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: a Unitree robot dog in the UK starts at €2,576 excluding VAT (Go2 Air) and goes up to €104,182 (B2-W with LiDAR). The Go2 and AS2 cover education, research and professional use; the A2 and B2 are industrial quadrupeds: the A2 with about 25 kg of payload and IP56/IP67, the B2 with over 40 kg and IP67. The robot dog used by Italy's Carabinieri is not a Unitree: according to the press it is a Boston Dynamics quadruped.

Anyone searching for the price of a robot dog is usually weighing up a quadruped for a lab, a company or a demo, and runs into two categories that should not be confused: remote-controlled toys and real quadrupeds. This article covers the latter, the Unitree models that Abra Robotics supplies in the UK, Ireland and internationally, with support in English. Prices start at €2,576 excluding VAT (Go2 Air) and go up to €104,182 (B2-W with LiDAR), according to the public price list.

Go2: the entry point for education and development

The Go2 is the consumer/education quadruped of the range. It weighs about 15 kg with the battery and carries a wide-angle 4D L2 LiDAR, but it has no declared IP rating: it suits indoor use, labs and demos, not rain or dust.

VersionPrice (excl. VAT)
Go2 Air€2,576
Go2 Pro€3,981
Go2 EDU€8,804
Go2 EDU with Livox Mid-360 LiDAR€16,875

The Go2 Air (€2,576) is the base model, with a 6-month warranty. The Pro adds the vector positioning module and voice control. The EDU is the development version, with a 12-month warranty and full SDK access. For 360° mapping there is the Go2 EDU configuration with Livox Mid-360 LiDAR, at €16,875. The profiles are compared in Go2 vs Go2 Pro. Note: the Go2 is not a toy, but it is still a consumer model. If you need IP54 or more payload, move up to the AS2.

The Carabinieri robot dog: what law enforcement uses

Italy's Carabinieri use a robot dog called Saetta, assigned to the bomb disposal unit (Nucleo Artificieri): it was presented in Rome in December 2023 and later arrived in Puglia, at the Bari bomb disposal unit, in 2026. According to Radio Laser it is not a Unitree robot but a Boston Dynamics quadruped. This is worth knowing so you do not confuse the platforms: the law-enforcement quadruped is not part of the Unitree range and has its own prices and supply chain.

Realistic robot dog or toy? How to tell them apart

Searches for "toy robot dog" and "realistic robot dog" tend to overlap. The test is simple: a toy is a remote-controlled plastic device, with no SDK, no LiDAR, no protection, costing a few tens of euros. A real quadruped has a measurable payload (over 40 kg on the B2), a LiDAR for navigation, ROS/ROS 2 and an SDK to program it, and an IP rating on the A2 and B2. If the price is under €1,000, it is almost always a toy.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a robot dog cost?

A Unitree quadruped starts at €2,576 excluding VAT (Go2 Air) and goes up to €104,182 (B2-W with LiDAR). The professional AS2 range goes from €5,400 to €21,277 (AS2 Pro on request); the industrial A2 and B2 from €30,814 to €104,182.

What is the difference between a toy robot dog and a professional one?

The toy is a remote-controlled device with four legs, with no SDK and no protection from dust or water. A Unitree quadruped has a real payload (over 40 kg on the B2), 4D LiDAR, ROS/ROS 2 and an SDK, and an IP56/IP67 protection rating on the industrial models.

What do the Carabinieri use?

Italy's Carabinieri use Saetta, a quadruped assigned to the bomb disposal unit. It is not a Unitree robot: according to the local press that described it, it is a Boston Dynamics quadruped.

Which robot dog should you choose for a university or lab?

Go2 EDU (€8,804) or AS2 EDU (from €12,473): ROS/ROS 2 SDK and an optional NVIDIA Jetson Orin computing module. With the Livox Mid-360 LiDAR the Go2 EDU costs €16,875.

Which robot dog for industrial inspection?

A2 (IP56, 25 kg payload) for semi-outdoor plants, B2 (IP67, over 40 kg) for construction sites and harsh environments. The AS2 Pro (IP54) covers indoor and perimeter surveillance.
