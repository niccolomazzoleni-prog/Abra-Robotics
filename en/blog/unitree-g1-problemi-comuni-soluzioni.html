Unitree G1: most common problems and how to fix them

Updated 22 November 2026Abra Robotics
In short: for almost every G1 problem, the first check is the Unitree Explore app, which shows motor status and errors. If the robot is not stable, the first thing to do is a full calibration from the app. If the problem persists, write to us.

Below are the problems we see most often on the G1, one per section: what to check, in what order, and when to stop. For the remote commands, see the G1 operating guide.

1. The robot does not balance, shakes or does not stand up

  1. Check the app. Unitree Explore shows the status of each motor and any errors: look for a joint in error or much hotter than the others.
  2. Check the start-up. The robot must start seated on a chair or hanging from the frame, and the key sequence must be the one on the sticker for your firmware version. For starts from lying down you need a flat, hard floor.
  3. Do a full body calibration from the app. It is the most effective fix when the robot is not balanced. If it does not work on the first attempt, repeat it a few times. Even if it works, repeat it once more to consolidate the result.
  4. If the robot is still unstable after a few calibrations, stop and write to us through the contact form.

2. A motor shows an error or gets hot

In the app, check which joint is in error and its temperature. Then stop the robot (seated or hanging, then damping), let it cool down and note the error code and the joint.

The code tells you which protection tripped, not which part is broken. The meanings are in the error table in the Unitree Document Center; the most common ones on the motor are overcurrent, overheating and encoder error.

If you develop with the SDK: high gains and arms held extended for a long time make the motors heat up, as in the case of the shoulder with an Inspire hand discussed in issue #129.

3. The app does not connect to the robot

  • The phone must really be on the G1's network (the robot's hotspot) or on the same Wi-Fi network, and stay close to the robot during pairing.
  • Bluetooth on, and Bluetooth permission granted to the app for the whole pairing.
  • Restart the app and the robot.
  • In our experience the app works better on Android than on iPhone: if it does not connect with an iPhone, try an Android phone.

4. The camera is not visible in the app

Video only gets through if the connection can handle the kilobytes per second the robot transmits. With a weak signal or a crowded network the rest of the app works but the image does not arrive or freezes. Move closer to the robot or use a less loaded Wi-Fi network.

5. The robot switches off suddenly

If the battery switches off with the red LED during a test, the overcurrent protection may have tripped: it happens with abrupt movements, for example going suddenly from a static pose to a trained policy. A Unitree staff member explains it in issue #14 of unitree_rl_mjlab: more gradual commands avoid the problem.

The battery is charged outside the robot, with the original charger. If it flashes red while charging, do not insist and do not open it. Spare battery: G1 battery, €880Unitree G1 Battery€880 excl. VATView product → excl. VAT.

When to call us

  • the robot stays unstable after a few calibrations;
  • an error comes back after a restart;
  • a joint heats up abnormally, jams or makes new noises;
  • the battery swells or does not accept charge;
  • after a fall there are cracks, exposed cables or loose connectors.

Write to us through the contact form with: serial number, firmware version, error code, the joint involved and, if you can, a video.

Frequently asked questions

The G1 does not balance: what do I do?

Check the motor status and errors in the app, then do a full calibration from the app. If needed, repeat it a few times. If the robot stays unstable, write to us through the contact form.

Why does the app not connect to the G1?

The phone must be on the robot's network, close by, with Bluetooth on and permission granted to the app. In our experience the app works better on Android.

Why can't I see the camera in the app?

Video needs more bandwidth than the rest of the app: with a weak signal the image does not arrive. Move closer to the robot or use a less loaded network.

How do I exit debug mode?

By restarting the robot.

What does a motor error mean?

It shows which protection tripped, for example overcurrent or overheating. Stop the robot, let it cool down and note the code and the joint.
