Unitree H2 Plus: price, specs and differences vs H2

14 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: the H2 Plus is the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T reference configuration of the full-size H2 humanoid. Same body (31 degrees of freedom, about 182 cm, about 70 kg), but with the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor T5000 compute module on board, two five-finger Sharpa Wave tactile hands and the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T stack. It is on pre-order: Unitree and NVIDIA indicate availability for late 2026.

Unitree H2 Plus is the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T reference configuration of Unitree's full-size humanoid: the same body as the H2 with three additions, the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor T5000 compute module on board, two five-finger Sharpa Wave tactile hands and the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T software stack. NVIDIA and Unitree announced it on 31 May 2026; it is on pre-order, with availability expected in late 2026.

Here you will find what really sets the Plus apart from the H2 and the H2 Air (official Unitree data only), how much it costs and when it arrives. For the full overview of the range see the H2 page; for prices across the whole family, the public price list.

What is the Unitree H2 Plus

The H2 Plus is the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T reference configuration for humanoid robot research, presented by NVIDIA and Unitree on 31 May 2026 at GTC Taipei. The NVIDIA press release describes it as an open reference design that combines the H2 body, the Sharpa Wave hands and the Jetson Thor compute with the Isaac GR00T software, from data capture through to policy training and deployment.

In practice, the H2 Plus is the H2 configured for work on foundation models and fine manipulation: the Thor compute, the Sharpa Wave hands and the Isaac GR00T stack in a single order. On the Unitree product page, however, the Sharpa Wave hands are listed as optional: the NVIDIA reference design includes them, so the actual configuration should be confirmed when ordering.

The Jetson AGX Thor T5000 module integrates a Blackwell GPU with 2,070 TFLOPS FP4 and 128 GB of unified memory, for on-board sensor processing and real-time model inference. For the Sharpa Wave hands, Unitree states more than 1,000 tactile pixels per fingertip and a force sensing resolution of 0.005 N.

Differences between H2 Plus, H2 and H2 Air

The body is identical across the versions. The differences are in compute, hands, software and availability. Watch the names: on the Unitree website the base version is called H2 and the development version H2 EDU; in the Abra price list you will find them as H2 Air (or H2 Basic) and H2 EDU.

In short: if you need to show the humanoid or run a first test, the H2 Air is enough. If you need to develop your own code on it, the H2 EDU adds the development PC and a choice of hands. The H2 Plus is the next step up, when you need compute for AI models and tactile manipulation with a stack that is already validated.

Unitree H2 Plus price and availability

The H2 Plus is on pre-order, with availability expected in late 2026. In the public price list the H2 Plus page shows €149,900 excluding VAT, as an indicative price with an updated quote on request.

Who the H2 Plus is for

The H2 Plus is designed for labs and universities working on physical AI and foundation models: it is the Isaac GR00T reference platform and brings compute, tactile hands and software together in a single system. The NVIDIA press release names Ai2, ETH Zurich, the Stanford Robotics Center and the Advanced Robotics and Controls Laboratory at UC San Diego among the first users.

For any other kind of purchase, it is often not the right choice. A demo or showroom is covered by the H2 Air; a lab that needs to write its own control stack finds everything it needs in the H2 EDU, minus the GR00T platform. And if the budget is tighter, a Unitree G1 EDU costs less and is already widely used by research labs: the comparison is in our 2026 humanoid comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Unitree H2 Plus?

It is the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T reference configuration of the full-size H2 humanoid, presented on 31 May 2026. Compared with the H2 it adds the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor T5000 compute on board, two five-finger Sharpa Wave tactile hands and the Isaac GR00T software stack. The body is the same as the H2: 31 degrees of freedom, about 182 cm and about 70 kg.

How much does the Unitree H2 Plus cost?

It is on pre-order for late 2026. In the public Abra price list the H2 Plus page shows €149,900 excluding VAT as an indicative price, with an updated quote on request. For context, the H2 family ranges from €35,363 (H2-A Standard A, upper body without legs) to €261,776 (H2 Plus Ultimate N), excluding VAT.

What is the difference between the H2 and the H2 Plus?

The body is the same (31 degrees of freedom). Three things change: the compute, with the Jetson AGX Thor T5000 as standard on the Plus; the hands, two tactile Sharpa Wave hands with 22 degrees of freedom each, bringing the total to 75; and the software, with Isaac GR00T integrated. The H2 Air and H2 EDU are available now; the Plus is expected in late 2026.

When is the Unitree H2 Plus released?

Unitree and NVIDIA indicate availability for late 2026. As of October 2026, the date of this article, it is on pre-order.

Who is the Unitree H2 Plus for?

For labs and universities working on physical AI and foundation models: it is the Isaac GR00T reference platform, with compute and tactile hands in a single validated system. For demos, showrooms or a first PoC it is worth considering the H2 Air or the G1 EDU, which are available now and cost less.
