Unitree Go2 EDU: what it includes, versions and prices

17 October 2026Abra Robotics
In brief: the Go2 EDU is the Go2 version with secondary development: the Air and Pro do not support it. It has a 15000 mAh battery (2-4 hours), a fast charger, a depth camera and foot force sensors. List prices run from €8,804 (EDU Standard) to €18,484 (EDU Ultimate with Hesai XT16 LiDAR). The versions differ in the computing module (Orin Nano 40 TOPS or Orin NX 100 TOPS) and in the additional 3D LiDAR.

Anyone searching for "unitree go2 edu" usually has two questions to answer: what the EDU version has inside compared with the others, and which EDU configuration to order. This page covers both, using only data from the official Unitree specifications table and the Abra Robotics price list. If the comparison you need is Go2 Air against Go2 Pro, it is not here: you will find it in the article Go2 vs Go2 Pro.

What the Go2 EDU includes

The EDU column of the Unitree Go2 specifications table shows what sets the EDU apart from the Air and Pro versions:

  • 15000 mAh long-endurance battery, with a stated run time of 2-4 hours (the Air and Pro have 8000 mAh, 1-2 hours).
  • Fast 33.6 V 9 A charger, instead of the standard 33.6 V 3.5 A one.
  • Depth camera included.
  • Foot force sensors: the only Go2 version that has them.
  • Remote control included (optional on the Air and Pro).
  • Full secondary development, with a high-performance 8-core CPU and the option to fit a high-power computing module (Unitree mentions several brands and models, for example Orin with 40-100 TOPS).

Everything else is shared by the whole Go2 family: a body of about 15 kg with battery, 70 × 31 × 40 cm standing, and the 4D LiDAR L2 with a 360° × 96° hemispherical field of view and a minimum detection distance of 0.05 m. A payload of about 8 kg (maximum about 12 kg), speed of 0-3.7 m/s (up to about 5 m/s in the lab), a maximum slope of 40° and a 12-month warranty are the EDU figures, shared with the X version; the Air stays more limited (payload about 7 kg, 0-2.5 m/s, 30° slope, 6-month warranty).

What is the difference between the Go2 EDU Standard and the Go2 EDU Smart?

The difference is the computing module. The EDU Standard has an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 8GB with 40 TOPS; the EDU Smart a Jetson Orin NX 16GB with 100 TOPS. The body, the 15000 mAh battery, the 4D LiDAR L2 and the force sensors stay the same. In practice the Smart is the one to choose when you need to run heavier neural networks, vision or reinforcement learning policies on board: with 100 TOPS you can run models that are slower on the 40 TOPS Nano or do not run on it at all.

The LiDAR versions: Plus (Livox Mid-360) and Ultimate (Hesai XT16)

The LiDAR configurations start from the Smart's computing (Orin NX 100 TOPS) and add a 3D sensor for mapping. The EDU Plus carries the Livox Mid-360 for SLAM and navigation; the EDU Ultimate carries the Hesai XT16, for high-resolution perception outdoors and in industrial use. Both include the RealSense D435i depth camera. In the price list the Livox Mid-360 LiDAR kit on its own costs from €4,402 and the Hesai XT16 kit from €6,603, which explains the price steps between the Smart, Plus and Ultimate.

SDK and development: how to program the Go2 EDU

Secondary development is the reason to buy the EDU: the Air and Pro do not support it. Unitree publishes two official tools. unitree_sdk2 is the C++ SDK, with examples ready to compile. unitree_ros2 exposes the robot's messages as ROS 2 topics via CycloneDDS, and its README lists support for Go2, B2 and H1, with Ubuntu 22.04 + ROS 2 Humble recommended and Ubuntu 20.04 + Foxy tested. The first steps, the 192.168.123.x network and example topics are in our quick ROS 2 guide for Unitree.

Go2 EDU or another version?

If you need to write code or integrate the robot into a ROS 2 stack, you need the EDU, full stop. For use with the app and remote control there are the Air and Pro, which cost less but have no secondary development: the full comparison is in Go2 vs Go2 Pro. The four EDU configurations are collected in the Unitree Go2 hub.

A note on the range: alongside the Go2, the price list includes the AS2, whose EDU versions follow the same logic (Standard, Smart, Plus with Mid-360 LiDAR and Ultimate). For labs and universities see Universities and Research.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the Go2 EDU Standard and the Go2 EDU Smart?

The computing module. The EDU Standard has an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 8GB with 40 TOPS, the Smart a Jetson Orin NX 16GB with 100 TOPS. The 15 kg body, the 15000 mAh battery and the 4D LiDAR L2 are the same in both versions.

How much does the Unitree Go2 EDU cost?

In the Abra Robotics price list, excluding VAT, with shipping and insurance quoted in the offer: EDU Standard from €8,804, EDU Smart from €12,473, EDU Plus with Livox Mid-360 LiDAR from €16,875, EDU Ultimate with Hesai XT16 LiDAR from €18,484.

Can the Go2 EDU be programmed with the SDK and ROS 2?

Yes. It is the Go2 version with secondary development: the Air and Pro do not support it. Unitree publishes unitree_sdk2 (C++) and unitree_ros2, whose README recommends Ubuntu 22.04 with ROS 2 Humble and lists Ubuntu 20.04 with Foxy as tested.

What does the Go2 EDU include compared with the Air and Pro?

A 15000 mAh long-endurance battery (2-4 hours instead of 1-2), a fast 33.6 V 9 A charger, a depth camera, foot force sensors, the remote control and secondary development. The body of about 15 kg and the 4D LiDAR L2 are common to the whole family.

Which LiDAR do the EDU LiDAR versions carry?

The Plus and Ultimate configurations start from the Orin NX 100 TOPS computing and add a 3D LiDAR: the Livox Mid-360 on the Plus, the Hesai XT16 on the Ultimate, both with a RealSense D435i depth camera. They are meant for SLAM and precise 3D mapping.
