Which Unitree AS2 should I choose?

AS2 Air: entry version with HD camera, for demos and getting started.

AS2 Pro / X: Unitree L2 LiDAR, IP54 and about 4 h endurance.

AS2 EDU: for development: industrial LiDAR and secondary development.

AS2-W: wheeled version.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree AS2 cost?

End-user prices for the Unitree AS2 start from € 5,400 excluding VAT and go up to € 29,900 depending on the version. Prices include shipping and duty to Italy; every order is confirmed with an updated quote.

Which Unitree AS2 version should I choose?

AS2 Air: entry version with HD camera, for demos and getting started. AS2 Pro / X: Unitree L2 LiDAR, IP54 and about 4 h endurance. AS2 EDU: for development: industrial LiDAR and secondary development. AS2-W: wheeled version.

What can the Unitree AS2 do?

The AS2 is Unitree's new-generation mid-size quadruped, between Go2 and A2, for research, inspection and demos.

Can I buy the Unitree AS2 outside Italy?

We mainly work in Italy, Switzerland and the Balkans; other EU countries on a project basis. Contact us for delivery and a quote for your country.
