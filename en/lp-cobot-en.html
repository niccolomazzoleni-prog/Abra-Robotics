FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which cobots can I purchase in Italy?

We distribute the Fairino FR Series cobot range: FR5, FR10 and FR20, configurable for your use case with software, training and support.

Are the Fairino cobots CE certified?

Yes, the entire range is CE certified and compliant with ISO 10218 and ISO/TS 15066.

Is a safety fence required?

No. Fairino cobots are designed for human-robot collaboration without barriers. Installation is possible directly on the production line.

How long from signing to delivery?

4 weeks for hardware supply.

Is the project eligible for financing?

We carry out a free eligibility check before proceeding. Most projects qualify for Industry 4.0, Transition 5.0 incentives or PNRR grants.

Do you provide technical support in Italy?

Yes, dedicated technical support in Italy from delivery to your ongoing project.
