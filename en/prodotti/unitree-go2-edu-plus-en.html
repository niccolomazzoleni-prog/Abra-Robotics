The robot

Why choose the Go2 EDU+

A development platform ready for research, inspection, and industrial POCs. Not a toy, not a surveillance robot: a professional tool.

For whom

Universities, R&D, AI teams

Universities, research laboratories, industrial R&D centers, AI and robotics development teams, companies looking to explore autonomous inspection or navigation in real environments.

What you can do with it

Six ready-to-use applications

  • Autonomous navigation and SLAM in industrial and outdoor environments
  • Inspection of facilities, warehouses, and perimeters (autonomous or teleoperated)
  • Research on locomotion, embodied AI, and multi-sensor fusion
  • Custom algorithm development with ROS, Python, C++ on real hardware
  • POC to demonstrate mobile automation to clients or investors
  • Integration with robotic arm (servo arm) for manipulation tasks

EDU+ vs Enterprise

Development, not surveillance

The Enterprise family is optimized for surveillance and remote communication — it does not support custom programming. The Go2 EDU+ is a development platform: open SDK, Jetson Orin NX 100 TOPS, full ROS support, expandable accessories. If you want to write code or conduct research, you need the EDU version.

EDU+ vs EDU base

More computing power, more battery life, more payload

The EDU+ features the Jetson Orin NX 16GB at 100 TOPS (vs Orin Nano 8GB at 40 TOPS of the EDU base), a 15000 mAh long endurance battery (2–4h battery life vs standard), higher maximum payload (12 kg vs 10 kg), and full compatibility with all accessories including the servo arm and additional LiDARs.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions

What is the difference between Go2 EDU and Go2 EDU+?

The EDU+ features Jetson Orin NX 16GB 100 TOPS (vs 40 TOPS of the EDU), a 15000 mAh long endurance battery, 12 kg max payload, and full compatibility with all expandable accessories. If you are planning serious research activities, the EDU+ is the right starting point.

What is the difference between Go2 EDU+ and Go2 Enterprise?

The Enterprise family is optimized for security and surveillance and does not support custom development. The EDU+ is a development platform with open SDK and Jetson Orin. Completely different target audiences.

What accessories can I add?

Robotic servo arm, Intel D435i depth camera, HESAI XTi6 or MID-360 LiDAR, wireless charging pad. All configurable at time of order.

How long from order confirmation to delivery?

4–6 weeks. Exact timing confirmed at order based on availability.

Does it operate autonomously or does it require an operator?

Both modes. It can execute pre-programmed autonomous missions or be teleoperated in real time. With the open SDK, you can develop fully custom navigation logic.

Is financing available?

Often yes. It typically qualifies for Industry 4.0 incentives and grants for innovation and research. Free eligibility check before purchase.
