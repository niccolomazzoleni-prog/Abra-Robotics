What Unitree has shown

Unitree presented the H2-X in October 2026 with the video “Kung Fu Legend: Guardian”, focused on security and rescue. In the video the robot stops a knife-wielding attacker, breaks a car door, lifts a vehicle to free a trapped person and pulls a pedestrian away from an oncoming car. Unitree has not said which of these actions are autonomous.

On its official page Unitree describes it as a next-generation full-size humanoid: about 170 cm, 65+ kg, 31 degrees of freedom, two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries with magnetic charging and about 8 hours of runtime both standing and walking. The listed applications are security, manufacturing, commercial services and home services.

Unitree recommends keeping a safety distance of at least 2–3 metres from the robot and warns against dangerous modifications and unauthorised use. Security or rescue deployments need a site risk assessment before installation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree H2-X cost?

From €36,713 excl. VAT for the H2-X Air. The H2-X EDU with the SDK costs €54,057; with hands it ranges from €55,378 (Dex1-1 Plus grippers) to €103,947 (BrainCo Revo 3 U21), up to €118,621 for the Full Setup. The final price is confirmed in the written quote.

What is the difference between the H2-X and the H2?

The H2-X is more compact (about 170 cm and 65+ kg, against 182 cm and about 70 kg for the H2) and has two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries: about 8 hours even while walking, against about 3 hours for the H2. It has more arm torque (150 vs 120 N·m), an LED display in the head and magnetic charging.

How long does the H2-X battery last?

Unitree states about 8 hours both standing and in continuous walking, with two hot-swappable 20 Ah batteries (1.152 kWh) and magnetic body charging.

Which H2-X configuration should I choose?

Air for demonstrations and remote-controlled use; EDU to develop software with the SDK. Choose the hands for the task: Dex1-1 grippers for simple grasps, Revo 2 and Dex3-1 for basic manipulation, Inspire FTP and Dex5-1 for fine five-finger grasping, Revo 3 U21 and the Full Setup with tactile U21T hands for dexterity research.

When is the H2-X available?

The H2-X is on pre-order. The delivery date is confirmed in the quote after checking with Unitree: we do not promise dates before that confirmation.
