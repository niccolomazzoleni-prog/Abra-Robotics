Engineering

Built for applied research, not just for demos.

Go2 Pro brings quadruped robotics within reach of everyone: 360° 3D LiDAR perception, front HD camera and integrated 4G connectivity for remote control, in a 15 kg agile body that handles slopes and steps with ease.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades and the aluminum alloy frame delivers rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in industrial and university environments.
