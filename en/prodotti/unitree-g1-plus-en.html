What changes from the G1: 6 upgrades

According to Unitree, the G1+ keeps the G1's value for money and improves it in motion, perception and intelligence. In short: +2 neck degrees of freedom, +110% shoulder and waist motor torque, +43% maximum arm torque and continuous external power supply.

01

2-DoF head

Pitch from −25° to 36° and yaw ±110°: the head turns to observe the surroundings and interact more naturally.

02

Upgraded motors

Shoulder and waist: peak torque +110%, heat at the same torque −72%. Arms: peak torque +43%, heat −30%. Thighs: heat at the same torque −28%.

03

Dual vision system

Binocular camera with 60 mm baseline, 2 MP, centre ≥500 LW/PH, FOV H110° / V80° / D125°; wide-angle monocular camera 1920×1080 at 30 fps, FOV H117° / V67°. Stereo depth perception and an ultra-wide field of view.

04

Head touch sensors

Touch sensors integrated in the head (5 high-sensitivity points): a tap starts a conversation or triggers a motion.

05

External power supply

The G1+ can run on an external power supply (rear input DC 54.6 V, ≤7.4 A) for long research sessions and demos.

06

Microphones and speakers

6-microphone array (4 front, 2 rear) with DOA sound-source localisation, noise reduction and echo cancellation; supports external wireless microphones (charging case with 50 m range and 80 dB signal-to-noise ratio). Dual 10 W waist speakers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Unitree G1+ cost?

The G1+ is priced on request: it does not have an Abra list price yet. We send a written quote for the version you need (G1+ or G1+ EDU).

What is the difference between the G1+ and the G1?

According to Unitree, the G1+ adds 2 head degrees of freedom, stronger and cooler-running shoulder, waist and arm motors, a wider binocular dual vision system, head touch sensors, external power supply and a 6-microphone array with dual 10 W speakers.

What is the difference between the G1+ and the G1+ EDU?

The G1+ EDU adds custom software development, up to 45 degrees of freedom with optional dexterous hands and wrists, an optional 3-DoF waist, computing modules such as Orin, an arm load of approx. 4 kg (vs approx. 3 kg) and an 18-month warranty (vs 8).

Is the G1+ the same robot as the G1 EDU Plus?

No. The G1 EDU Plus (U2) is an EDU version of the current G1. The G1+ is the new upgraded model described on this page.
