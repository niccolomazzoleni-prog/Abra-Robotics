Engineering

Built for applied research, not just demos.

A2 Pro is designed for field autonomy: dual front and rear LiDAR, integrated GPS and 4G, and wireless vector positioning power SLAM and autonomous navigation, on a tri-processor architecture with protection up to IP67.

The modular architecture allows rapid upgrades and the aluminum alloy structure ensures rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in industrial and university environments.
