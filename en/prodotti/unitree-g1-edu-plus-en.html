Engineering

Built for applied research, not just demos.

G1 EDU Plus is the modular base for manipulation: the 3-DoF waist and 3-DoF wrists extend the arm workspace, while the standard interface allows Dex3, Dex5 or 5-finger Inspire hands to be fitted when the project requires it.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades, and the aluminium alloy structure ensures rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in industrial and university environments.

Capabilities

What it can do

The key capabilities of G1 EDU Plus — ready out of the box.

Articulated waist and wrists

Ready for dexterous hands
