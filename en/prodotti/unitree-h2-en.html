Request information about this product

The H2 is Unitree's full-size humanoid: compared to the previous generation, it prioritizes stability, fine motor control and articulation for manipulation tasks. Torque up to 360 N·m at the leg joints and 120 N·m at the arms, with expandable onboard AI computing (up to high-end NVIDIA Jetson modules on the EDU version). A platform to be deployed through a structured POC for advanced research and development.
