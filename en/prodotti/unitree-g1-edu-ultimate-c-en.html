Engineering

Built for applied research, not just demos.

G1 EDU Ultimate C brings anthropomorphic manipulation: the Inspire 5-finger hands, with integrated tactile sensors and 12 joints per hand, replicate human grasps and gestures for HRI research and real-world tasks.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades, while the aluminum alloy frame ensures rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in industrial and academic environments.
