Engineering

Built for real environments, not just the lab.

G1 Air is the result of years of R&D in applied humanoid robotics. Every joint is designed to last, every sensor chosen to perform in real-world conditions: variable lighting, uneven terrain, unexpected interactions.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades. The aluminum alloy frame ensures rigidity without compromising the total weight of 35 kg, making it portable and operational in industrial and university environments.

Capabilities

What G1 can do

From fluid motion to autonomous navigation - three key capabilities ready to use.

Built-in movement library

G1 includes a library of pre-loaded movements - walking, balance, fall recovery - ready to use without additional configuration.

Agility and impact resistance

The reinforced structure and dynamic control allow G1 to recover from pushes, unexpected obstacles, and unstable surfaces in real time.

360° autonomous navigation

The LIVOX MID-360 LiDAR and RealSense D435i depth camera provide complete environmental awareness for autonomous navigation even in crowded spaces.
