Engineering

Built for applied research, not just for demo.

G1 EDU Ultimate A brings three-finger manipulation: Dex3 hands, with 7 DoF each, grasp and manipulate complex-shaped objects, while the 42 total degrees of freedom provide a complete platform for manipulation and interaction research.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades and the aluminum alloy frame ensures rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in industrial and university environments.
