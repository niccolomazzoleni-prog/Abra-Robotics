Engineering

Built for applied research, not just demonstrations.

B2 is the powerhouse of the lineup: over 6 m/s speed, 120 kg static payload and 360 N·m joint torque, with IP67 rating and 32-channel automotive LiDAR. It clears over 1.6 m and handles the most demanding industrial environments, including with the Z1 arm.

The modular architecture enables rapid upgrades and the aluminum alloy frame ensures rigidity without compromising weight, keeping the robot portable and operational in both industrial and academic environments.
