FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does an AMR do?

An autonomous mobile robot moves materials between areas of the facility without physical guides, navigating autonomously and avoiding obstacles and people.

What is the difference between an AGV and an AMR?

An AGV follows fixed paths, while an AMR navigates autonomously and adapts to its environment, without the need for floor guides.

Do AMRs integrate with my ERP or MES?

Yes, we integrate with ERP and MES and manage the fleet, so the robot is a measurable asset coordinated with the rest of your logistics.

How disruptive is it to my current logistics?

We start with a pilot on a specific workflow, without disrupting the rest. The initial assessment is free.
