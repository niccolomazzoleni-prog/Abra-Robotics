FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is the official Unitree distributor in Italy?

Abra Robotics is the official Unitree distributor in Italy. We supply the full range — Go2, G1, B2 — with configuration, custom software, training and dedicated technical support for companies, universities and research institutes.

Can I purchase hardware only, without services?

Yes. We supply Unitree robots without a software package or training for clients who already have an in-house technical team. We deliver the robot configured and ready to use.

Are Unitree robots compatible with ROS2?

Yes. The entire Unitree range supports ROS2 natively. We also offer integration support, technical documentation and training for your development team.

How long does it take from signing to delivery?

Humanoids 4-8 weeks, quadrupeds 4-6 weeks, AMR and cobots 4 weeks. Lead times from signing to hardware delivery; deployment included in the subsequent phase.

Is the robotics project eligible for financing?

Most projects qualify for Industry 4.0 / Transition 5.0 incentives or regional PNRR grants. We conduct a free financing eligibility check before proceeding with the proposal.

Which Unitree robots are available for purchase in Italy?

We offer the full Unitree range: Go2 and Go2 Pro (quadrupeds for inspection and research), G1 (humanoid for manufacturing and R&D) and B2 (industrial quadruped IP67 for heavy environments). All configurable for your specific use case.

Do you operate only in Italy?

We primarily operate in Italy, with a focus on Northern Italy. For specific projects we also evaluate collaborations abroad.
