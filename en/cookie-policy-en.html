This page explains what cookies are, which tools are used by the Abra Robotics website, and how you can manage your preferences. It forms an integral part of the Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that visited websites send to the user's device, where they are stored and retransmitted to the same sites on subsequent visits. There are technical cookies (necessary for the website to function or to provide a service requested by the user) and profiling cookies (designed to build user profiles for advertising purposes). Only the latter require the user's prior consent.

2. Cookies and tools used by this site

This website uses exclusively technical tools and does not install profiling cookies or third-party analytics or advertising tools.
