02 · Quadrupeds

Continuous facility inspection. Without putting anyone at risk.

Unitree quadruped robots do what an operator shouldn't: they enter hazardous areas, work at night, collect thermal and acoustic data in real-time. The use case is solid when you have at least one of these conditions: periodic patrols in high-risk areas, recurring thermal inspections, 24/7 perimeter surveillance, automatic documentation for audits and predictive maintenance.

When it makes sense

  • Periodic patrols in high-risk or hard-to-reach areas
  • Recurring thermal or acoustic inspections on critical components
  • 24/7 perimeter surveillance without security guard costs
  • Structured data collection for predictive maintenance and audits
  • Site inspection in hazardous areas instead of operators
