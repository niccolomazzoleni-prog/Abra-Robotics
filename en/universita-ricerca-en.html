01 · Programmable platforms

Open platforms. Program at any level.

All platforms we carry expose complete SDKs: C++, Python, ROS2. From high-level to low-level control, every layer is accessible. EDU and research versions are the only choice for those who want to develop their own software: base models do not support secondary development.
