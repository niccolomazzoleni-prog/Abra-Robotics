Financing & Incentives 2026

Buying a robot in Italy in 2026: iperammortamento and Nuova Sabatini.

For Italian companies investing in robotics, the main 2026 measure is the iperammortamento (hyper-depreciation) introduced by the 2026 Budget Law and managed through filings with the GSE. SMEs can also consider the Nuova Sabatini. We help with the preliminary technical check; eligibility of each asset is confirmed by the sworn appraisal and your tax advisor.

01 · Iperammortamento 2026

The main measure for companies buying a robot in 2026.

An uplift of the asset's cost that increases the tax-deductible depreciation (or finance-lease instalments).

Legal basis

  • Law no. 199 of 30 December 2025 (2026 Budget Law), article 1, paragraphs 427-436.
  • Interministerial decree of 7 May 2026 (MIMIT-MEF) setting the implementing rules.
  • Decree-Law no. 38 of 27 March 2026, article 7 (converted by Law no. 88 of 22 May 2026): it removed the requirement for assets to be produced in the EU or EEA, with effect from 1 January 2026. A robot made outside the EU is therefore not excluded because of its origin.

Main requirements

  • Who: companies resident in Italy and Italian permanent establishments of non-resident entities, regardless of legal form, sector and size.
  • When: investments made from 1 January 2026 to 30 September 2028.
  • Which assets: new tangible and intangible capital goods listed in Annexes IV and V of Law 199/2025, interconnected with the company's production management system or supply network.
  • Documents: a sworn technical appraisal and an accounting certification confirming that the investment is real and compliant.

How large is the uplift

Investment trancheCost uplift
Up to €2.5 million180%
Over €2.5 million and up to €10 million100%
Over €10 million and up to €20 million50%

The iperammortamento is not a tax credit and cannot be offset via the F24 form: it reduces taxable income over the depreciation years, so the company needs taxable profit. The benefit is neither automatic nor guaranteed: eligibility of each robot is confirmed by the technical appraisal and your tax advisor.

02 · GSE procedure

Three mandatory filings on the GSE platform.

They are submitted from the GSE customer area with SPID or CIE, using the forms approved by the MIMIT directorial decrees of 10 June 2026 and 20 July 2026.

1Booking

Preliminary communication

Filed for each production site: company details, type and amount of the planned investments and, for each asset, the expected completion and interconnection dates. Open since 12 June 2026.

2Investment start

Confirmation communication

Within 60 days of the positive outcome of the preliminary communication. It confirms the booked assets (none can be added and costs can only decrease) and certifies down payments of at least 20% of each asset's cost, with invoice details. Open since 21 July 2026.

3Closing

Completion communication

Once the investment is completed and no later than 15 November 2028: completion and interconnection dates and a declaration that the appraisal and accounting certification are available. The uplift applies from the tax period in which it is filed, after a positive GSE check.

+Following years

Periodic communications

According to the GSE instructions, periodic filings in January and June with the expected use of the benefit and the depreciation plan. Deadlines and forms are updated by directorial decrees: check the official pages listed under Sources before each filing.

03 · Nuova Sabatini

For SMEs buying with a loan or a lease.

A Ministry grant covering the interest on a bank loan or lease for capital goods.

  • Who: micro, small and medium-sized enterprises with a registered office or local unit in Italy.
  • Financing: from €20,000 to €4 million, maximum term 5 years, granted by a participating bank or financial intermediary (leasing included).
  • Grant: equal to the conventional interest on a 5-year loan at 2.75% for ordinary investments and 3.575% for digital and green investments, according to the MIMIT page.
  • Funding: the 2026 Budget Law refinanced the measure with €650 million for 2026 and 2027 (€200 million for 2026 and €450 million for 2027). Applications go through the bank.

Iperammortamento and Nuova Sabatini on the same asset: the official MIMIT pages of the two measures do not state that they can be combined. We do not take it for granted: check it case by case with your bank and tax advisor.

05 · Other tools

Grants, leasing and subsidised finance.

  • INAIL ISI call: non-repayable grants for projects that reduce risks for workers. The 2025 edition covered up to 65% of eligible costs, with a maximum of €130,000 per project. Rules and dates change with each edition.
  • Finance lease: instalments instead of an upfront payment; according to the MIMIT page the iperammortamento uplift also applies to finance-lease instalments. With operating rental the asset stays with the rental company: check with your advisor who can benefit from incentives.
  • SIMEST and regional calls: subsidised loans for companies with foreign business and regional calls for digitalisation and automation. Terms and windows are published by SIMEST and by each Region.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can a robot made outside the EU qualify for the 2026 iperammortamento?

Yes, its origin does not exclude it: article 7 of Decree-Law 38/2026 removed the EU/EEA production requirement with effect from 1 January 2026. The other requirements remain (new asset, listed in Annex IV or V of Law 199/2025, interconnected). Confirmation for each asset is up to the appraiser and your tax advisor.

Is the iperammortamento a tax credit?

No. It is an uplift of the asset's cost that applies only for tax purposes to depreciation and finance-lease instalments. It cannot be offset via F24: it reduces taxable income over the depreciation years.

When are the GSE filings due?

The preliminary communication books the benefit; the confirmation communication is due within 60 days of the positive outcome of the preliminary one, with down payments of at least 20% of each asset's cost; the completion communication by 15 November 2028. It is best to plan them before signing the order.

Can the iperammortamento and Nuova Sabatini be used together?

The official MIMIT pages of the two measures do not state that they can be combined. We do not take it for granted: check it case by case with your bank and tax advisor.

Do these incentives apply to leasing?

For the iperammortamento, the MIMIT page states that the uplift also applies to finance-lease instalments; the Nuova Sabatini also covers leasing. Operating rental needs a check with your advisor.

Do I need to have chosen the robot before the check?

No. It is better to talk before choosing the model: interconnection depends on the robot version and its intended use. We help you choose a configuration that can be documented.

I already have an accountant. Why should I talk to you?

Because we know the technical requirements of the robots we sell (interconnection, asset classification, documentation for the appraiser). Technical and tax checks go together: we provide the technical material, the tax confirmation stays with your accountant.

Does Abra Robotics guarantee access to incentives?

No. We carry out a free preliminary technical check, but eligibility and the amount of the benefit depend on your company and are confirmed by the sworn technical appraisal and your tax advisor.
